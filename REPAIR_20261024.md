## Actual functionality

Location sampling targets 15 seconds through one fused-provider callback during authorized SOS, circle and journey sessions. WorkManager only synchronizes deferred work. Fresh acquisition has a timeout and an explicitly cached fallback. Coordinates, finite accuracy, capture time and staleness are validated. Denied permission, disabled GPS and unavailable location produce recovery information. Ending the last session stops sampling. Visible-app resume can restore authorized sessions; force-stop, permission removal, battery restrictions and reboot restrictions are not bypassed.

The encrypted location journal holds at most 240 points per owner and prunes points older than 24 hours when saving new samples. Latest uploads are monotonic. Each incident has 60 rolling minute-history slots. A continuous session can generate approximately 240 latest-location and 60 history writes/hour, plus transaction/subscriber reads. Cloud confirmation is distinct from recipient viewing. Free quotas remain project-wide.

Native SMS uses configured SIM/carrier service, validated personal recipients, available genuine coordinates/map links, and real submission/delivery callbacks. Durable recipient claims prevent blind duplicate submission. Empty recipients, disabled SMS, failure and unavailable delivery reports remain explicit. No carrier SMS was sent by tests. Public helplines open the native dialer; no emergency calls were placed. India 112 and regional helpline qualifications were checked against official sources.

Circle relationships use authenticated UIDs and single-use 15-minute codes. Membership alone grants no location access. Directional sharing grants last at most one hour and bind to the relationship version; revocation/reacceptance cannot restore an old grant. Private phone/DOB/address remain owner-only. Public projections contain real name/photo; no fabricated verification or online presence is added. Notifications use authorized active listeners and refresh-on-resume. Spark has no automatic trusted FCM sender here; killed/offline receiver delivery is not guaranteed.

Groups support up to 32 members, actual profile projections, expiring 128-bit invitations, immutable messages up to 1,000 characters, 40-message pagination and encrypted local pending messages. Cloud confirmation and peer receipt remain distinct. Owner removal rotates the nearby key. Member departure pauses cloud sends until the owner rotates it. Cached keys/data on disconnected former members cannot be erased remotely. Cloud creation/join requires internet; an already joined group can use BLE offline. Manual pairing is labelled offline-only and grants no cloud membership. Owner transfer/group deletion is not implemented; an owner cannot leave its group.

Nearby transport is opportunistic BLE GATT with authenticated/encrypted packets, expiry, deduplication, bounded queueing, hop limits and actual ACK handling. It depends on compatible apps, a shared private group, permissions, radios and permitted execution. No fake peers/ACKs were added. One phone cannot certify successful packet transfer, remote disconnect handling or multi-hop coverage.

## Approved deletion and migration

The user explicitly approved expanded deletion after automatic review initially blocked it. History requires confirmation before deleting an owner's resolved/cancelled incident. It synchronizes terminal state, records a private opaque deletion ID/time, removes location children in bounded batches, then deletes the shared summary and private audit/mirror entries. A matching latest-location record is removed; unrelated sessions remain. Local journal/outbox cleanup follows confirmed cloud completion.

Errors/timeouts explain that deletion may be partial and allow retry with the same ID. Deletion IDs block delayed workers or another device from restoring deleted records; they contain no coordinates, profile or message. Already received copies cannot be recalled. No production history was deleted as part of testing.

Existing collection names, profiles and relationships are retained. New location writes must include an incident/session ID, including circle/journey. Older clients omitting this field must upgrade. Automatic approval review rejected making this optional because that would bypass deletion protection; the stricter boundary remains. Existing records remain readable under their prior authorization. Existing grants without a relationship version require explicit re-enabling. No bulk production migration was performed.

## Remaining limits

- Two physical phones: successful BLE transfer, receiver persistence/ACK, radio-off/reconnect/disconnect behavior and actual cross-device account/profile UI. Three phones: A→B→C relay. Sequential accounts on one phone do not certify these.
- Real SIM submission/delivery, dual-SIM behavior and carrier failures remain untested.
- Android 16 was tested. Other Android versions, vendor battery policies, sustained background sessions and reboot recovery need a device matrix. Force-stopped operation is unsupported.
- No paid geocoding/routing/ETA, automatic FCM server trigger, global invitation abuse service or guaranteed mesh coverage. OSM tiles are online, policy-limited and have no SLA.
- Cloud group messages, expired invitations and deletion IDs have no automatic retention cleanup. Authorized maintenance is required. Incident deletion is not account-wide deletion.
- Existing lint/deprecation warnings remain. This debug-signed APK uses the existing target SDK; it is not Play release certification.
