Why SafeAlerts

Built for real emergencies, not fair-weather demos.

When panic hits, cellular data drops, or you cannot unlock your phone fast enough, conventional messaging apps fail. SafeAlerts combines redundant hardware triggers and honest telemetry so help reaches the people who care.

🚨

Multi-Channel Instant SOS & Real SMS

Trigger an alert via the 1-tap/2-second hold SOS button, 3x physical shake in the background, or Voice "HELP ME" recognition. SafeAlerts attaches your exact GPS coordinates, creates a Google Maps link, and dispatches native cellular SMS alongside Firebase Cloud audit sync.

SmsManager Cellular Dispatch 3s Countdown Guard Medical / Accident / Fire / Security
🧭

Women's Safety Journey & Check-In Timers

Search real destinations with Google Places Autocomplete, visualize the route & ETA on Google Maps, and activate an arrival timer with configurable grace periods. If you don't confirm arrival in time, SafeAlerts escalates automatically with your live coordinates and destination details.

Google Places Search OSRM Route & ETA 1091 Helpline Quick Dial
📡

Zero-Internet BLE Mesh

No Wi-Fi or cell tower? Broadcast signed SOS packets and encrypted text chat directly to nearby paired SafeAlerts phones over Bluetooth Low Energy.

Offline SOS Beacon BLE Encrypted Chat
👥

Consent-Based Trusted Circle

Link family and close friends via single-use invitation codes. View live member pins on Google Maps with automatic 1-hour expiry and stale-fix warnings.

Mutual Invite Codes Firestore Realtime
🔍

Honest Telemetry & Audit History

Never fakes a delivery receipt. Inspect exact SMS, Cloud, GPS accuracy (±m), and permission readiness for every single incident in Alert History.
